# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

Azan is a prayer-times, Quran, and reminders app. This policy explains what
information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information used by the app

- **Location:** With your permission, Azan obtains the device's location to
  identify your city and calculate local prayer times and Qibla direction. The
  app stores the selected city and latitude/longitude on your device. When
  retrieving prayer times online, it sends the coordinates to the AlAdhan API
  (`api.aladhan.com`). If location access is unavailable or denied, the app
  uses its default Cairo location unless you have previously saved another
  location.
- **Reminders and preferences:** Reminder titles, dates, times, repeat settings,
  completion status, prayer notification preferences, and app selections are
  stored locally on your device. The app schedules notifications on the device.
- **Quran content:** When Quran text is not already cached on your device, the
  app requests the selected Surah from UmmahAPI (`ummahapi.com`) and caches the
  response locally. Quran audio is streamed from the audio hosts selected by
  the app, including EveryAyah (`everyayah.com`) and MP3Quran (`mp3quran.net`).
- **Prayer-time calculation:** Prayer times are requested from AlAdhan when an
  internet connection is available. If the request fails, the app calculates
  them locally on the device.

Requests to these services may expose standard connection data, such as your
IP address, to the service receiving the request. Their handling of that data
is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## Sharing and sale

Azan does not provide an account service and does not send your reminders or
locally stored preferences to an Azan-operated server. The app does not sell
personal information. Information is sent to the service providers described
above only as needed to provide the requested location-based prayer times,
Quran text, or audio streaming.

## Retention and your choices

App data is stored locally. You can deny location permission in Android
settings; location-based features may then use a saved or default location.
You can delete reminders within the app. Clearing Azan's storage or
uninstalling it removes its local app data, subject to Android device backup
settings. Network requests stop when you stop using the corresponding online
features.

## Children

Azan is a general audience religious utility and is not designed to collect
personal information from children. It does not require registration.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version and its
effective date will be published at this page.
