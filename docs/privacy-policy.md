# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 15, 2026**

Azan is a prayer-times, Quran, and reminders app. This policy explains what
information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information used by the app

- **Location:** With your permission, Azan obtains the device's location to
  identify your city and calculate local prayer times and Qibla direction. The
  app stores the selected city and latitude/longitude on your device. When
  retrieving prayer times online, it sends the coordinates to the AlAdhan API
  (`api.aladhan.com`). If location access is unavailable or denied, the app
  uses its default Cairo location unless you have previously saved another
  location.
- **Reminders and preferences:** Reminder titles, dates, times, repeat settings,
  completion status, prayer notification preferences, and app selections are
  stored locally on your device. The app schedules notifications on the device.
- **Quran content:** When Quran text is not already cached on your device, the
  app requests the selected Surah from UmmahAPI (`ummahapi.com`) and caches the
  response locally. Quran audio is streamed from the audio hosts selected by
  the app, including EveryAyah (`everyayah.com`) and MP3Quran (`mp3quran.net`).
- **Prayer-time calculation:** Prayer times are requested from AlAdhan when an
  internet connection is available. If the request fails, the app calculates
  them locally on the device.
- **Optional account:** Online worship features use Supabase to store your
  email, authentication credentials, display name and friend code. The app
  retains your session on your device. Email confirmation and password reset
  messages are delivered through the configured email provider.
- **Online worship:** Room membership, invitations and join requests, daily
  task completions and weekly reports are stored in Supabase. Accepted room
  members can see each other's display names and weekly task counts, points
  and areas to improve. Room owners can see and approve join requests. Share
  room references and friend codes only with people you intend to invite.
- **Profile photo:** If you choose a photo, it is uploaded to private Supabase
  Storage, with a limit of 2 MB per image. Access requires your authenticated
  account; profile display uses a temporary signed link.
- **Contact:** Contact Us opens your email app. Your email address and message
  are sent to the publisher only when you send the draft in that app.

Requests to these services may expose standard connection data, such as your
IP address, to the service receiving the request. Their handling of that data
is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## Sharing and sale

Azan does not sell personal information. Local reminders and preferences are
not uploaded to the online worship service. Account and online worship data
are shared with Supabase to provide the requested features, and the configured
email provider processes authentication emails. Room participants can see the
shared worship information described above. Other network services receive
information as needed for prayer times, Quran text and audio streaming.

## Retention and your choices

Offline app data is stored locally. You can deny location permission in Android
settings; location-based features may then use a saved or default location.
You can delete reminders within the app. Clearing Azan's storage or
uninstalling it removes its local app data, subject to Android device backup
settings. Network requests stop when you stop using the corresponding online
features.

Optional account, room and report data remains online when you uninstall the
app or clear its storage. To request deletion of your account and associated
online data, email `taemaomar65@gmail.com` from your registered email address.
Profile photo replacements overwrite the same image format; previous uploads
in other supported formats may remain until account data is removed by the
publisher. Signing out removes the active local session.

## Children

Azan is a general audience religious utility and is not designed to collect
personal information from children. Offline features do not require
registration; online worship features require an account.
